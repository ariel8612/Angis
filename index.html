How a Piece Comes Together

Two steps, and I handle both

We figure out the design

Tell me what you're picturing. A name, a date, a scene, your own artwork. My design artist lays it out on her computer, and once you like what you see, I start stitching.

I put it all together

Stockings and ornaments don't stop at the stitching. I work with a sewer who assembles them, so what you get back is finished and ready to hang.
