How a Piece Comes Together

It's a two-step process, and I handle both

We figure out the design

Tell me what you're picturing — a name, a date, a favorite memory, your own artwork. My design artist can lay it out on her computer, and once you're happy with it, I get stitching.

I put it all together

If you're going with a stocking or ornament, I work with a sewer to get it fully assembled and ready to use — not just stitched. We'll sort that part out once you reach out.
